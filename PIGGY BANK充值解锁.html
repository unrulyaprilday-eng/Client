Rules

Gullak Bonus is unlocked according to the recharge amount tier configured by the platform.

Unlock amount = a single recharge amount multiplied by the ratio of its tier.

主页面表现

  • 主视觉强调充值金额档位，而不是 VIP 等级。
  • Quick Actions 中间卡仅用于增加 Gullak Bonus；右侧与下方卡片仅用于充值解锁。

顶部按钮

  • ?：点击打开充值档位 Rules。

  • Claim：可领取金额为 0 时保持不可领取提示。
